        The drum words

        Taiko players learn every rhythm by saying it out loud first. Each word is an instruction: which hand, where to hit, how big, and for how long. Tap any word to see it on the drum and hear it.

        These are the words most groups share. Groups differ a little, so if a teacher says it another way, follow the teacher.

        The four words you already know

        Which hand, how big

        Almost every drum word fits this grid. Bigger hits use more of the arm. Two short words joined together make a pair, and the right hand usually goes first.

        All the words

        Each card shows one beat split into two halves. A dot is a hit: R or L is the hand, a bigger dot is a bigger hit, round is the skin and square is the rim. A tail means “let it ring”.

        Small marks that change a word

        ン

        Ends in n: let it ring

        ドン Don rings out. ド Do is short.

        ッ

        Small tsu: stop it short

        カッ Kat is a Ka that stops sharply.

        ー

        Long mark: hold it longer

        ドーン Dōn is a Don that rings for longer.

        間

        Ma: the space between

        Silence is part of the music. Count rests as carefully as hits.
